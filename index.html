Gulf Stocks — Version 1.3 privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026. Applies to version 1.3 when available. Provider: Hamam Alabdulla. Contact: hamam@gccapps.com.

Gulf Stocks is an independent personal watchlist and notes app with links to exchange stock pages. Version 1.3 displays no prices or scores and contains no advertising, purchases or advertising tracking. Local use needs no account.

Stock symbols, ordering, notes and language preferences remain on your device. Free cloud sync is optional with Sign in with Apple. Each account has a separate list; local research is copied into it only at your request and uploaded when you sync. Firebase stores account symbols, ordering, notes and creation/edit/add/removal timestamps under the account ID. Each account has a local cache; signing out returns to your local list without deleting the account's on-device cache.

Firebase Authentication uses Apple and Firebase identifiers to authenticate and secure accounts. Version 1.3 requests no Apple name or email scopes; account information provided in an earlier version may remain until account deletion. App Check processes app/device integrity evidence. Crashlytics processes crash diagnostics, device state and installation identifiers. We use neither Firebase Analytics nor the advertising identifier. Service providers may process network addresses and security logs to operate and protect requests.

Removing a stock hides it and retains its note and a removal record so stale devices cannot restore it during sync. To erase a note's text, clear and save it, then sync the account. Delete account requires Apple verification and removes the cloud watchlist, Firebase account and that account's saved list in the app on this device, preserving unrelated local research. Firebase SDK caches on this device and offline copies on other devices may remain until you delete the app and its data on those devices. We retain the deleted account ID and deletion time as a security marker preventing old tokens from recreating data; it contains no notes. Backups and diagnostics follow providers' retention cycles.

Exchange links open only when you choose them, in your browser. They contain no notes or account IDs. External websites receive browser/network information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not copy their data into the app or claim exchange affiliation.

We do not sell account data or notes. Apple and Google/Firebase may process data outside your country. Processing supports requested functionality, security and diagnostics. We use encrypted connections and account access rules; absolute security cannot be guaranteed. For access, correction, export or deletion requests contact hamam@gccapps.com. The app is not directed to children under 13. Policy changes will be identified when published.

Crashlytics reporting starts automatically when the app launches, including before sign-in. Version 1.3 has no in-app opt-in or switch to disable diagnostic collection.

Third-party providers that receive user data provide the same or equal protection described in this policy and required by the App Store Review Guidelines. Google/Firebase processes our app data under its data-processing and security terms, including confidentiality, security, deletion and subprocessor obligations. Apple protects Sign in with Apple data under its privacy commitments. External exchange websites you choose to visit operate under their own policies.

Earlier version 1.2: the August 8, 2026 policy at https://qtrcipher.github.io/gulf-stocks-privacy-policy/ describes that version's AdMob, UMP, ATT, StoreKit and former market/purchase processing. Removing components from 1.3 does not remove them from installed 1.2 copies. This policy does not change 1.2 availability or App Store products.

Gulf Stocks is not investment advice. Prices, charts, fundamentals, scores, and Buy/Hold/Sell labels are provided for personal research only.

What We Collect and Why

Data you provide or create

  • Watchlist stock symbols, display order, and personal notes.
  • Language preference and first-run preferences stored on the device.
  • Firebase user ID linked to Sign in with Apple when cloud sync is enabled.
  • Name or email if Apple provides it; the email may be an Apple Private Relay address.

We use this data to provide the watchlist, notes, sync, language settings, and restore features across your devices when sync is enabled.

Data processed automatically

  • Crash and diagnostic data from Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability.
  • App Check and security signals to protect backend APIs from abuse.
  • StoreKit subscription data to verify Gulf Stocks Pro access.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and approximate IP-derived location when Google AdMob ads are shown in the free version.

Firebase Analytics is not used; the app's Firebase configuration has analytics disabled.

Legal Basis

We process data to provide requested app functionality, manage subscriptions and sync, protect the service from abuse, meet platform requirements, and improve reliability. When law or device settings require consent, such as advertising consent or app tracking permission, the app asks before proceeding.

Cloud Sync

Cloud sync is optional and requires Sign in with Apple plus an active Gulf Stocks Pro subscription. When enabled, Firebase stores your watchlist, notes, creation dates, and note edit dates under your user ID. If you do not use cloud sync, watchlist and note data stay local to your device except for third-party processing described in this policy.

Advertising and Consent

The free version may show ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device and advertising identifiers, app or ad interactions, approximate location, and performance data to provide and measure ads. The app uses Google User Messaging Platform to manage ad consent where required and Apple's App Tracking Transparency before allowing tracking for advertising.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, StoreKit, and subscription processing.
  • Firebase: Authentication, Firestore, Functions, App Check, and Crashlytics.
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform: ads and consent management.

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your watchlist, notes, or account data.

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, delete the app, or reset app data. Cloud sync data remains while your account is active or until you request deletion. Subscription, fraud-prevention, or legally required records may be retained as needed for compliance and operations.

  • You can delete your account and cloud sync data from Settings while signed in.
  • You can contact us to request access, correction, export, or deletion of your data.
  • You can manage ad preferences through the app's privacy options when available and through iOS settings.

Security

We use Firebase, Apple, and Google services that transmit data over encrypted connections. Market data provider keys are kept in the backend instead of inside the iOS app. No electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

International Transfers

Apple, Google, Firebase, and infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than your country of residence, according to their policies and applicable legal requirements.
